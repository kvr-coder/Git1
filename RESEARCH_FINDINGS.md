# Market-entry research findings

Compiled Jun 16 2026 from a multi-agent deep-research run (~25 sub-reports,
every claim traced to a primary source where one exists). Pairs with
`MARKETING.md` (GTM strategy), `IDEAS.md` (product), `INFRA.md` (ops).

Scope: how a solo bootstrapped founder enters the crowded consumer
parental-control market with a PC-first, privacy-positioned product at ~$79/yr.

Confidence is flagged throughout. Where the public record is thin, that is
stated rather than papered over.

---

## 0. The single most important finding

**Every successful indie family-tech founder won by deeply embedding with ONE
credentialed gatekeeper — not by out-marketing incumbents.** This pattern is
consistent across all four well-documented cases (OurFamilyWizard, Gabb,
Troomi, Pinwheel) and matches the privacy-tools cases (Cryptee, one sec).

The corollary: **broad-market acquisition channels (SEO, paid ads, Show HN,
Product Hunt) do not work for a solo founder in this category.** The evidence
for this is strong and specific (below).

Your equivalent of OFW's family-court judge is **ADHD coaches, pediatricians,
child psychologists, and school counsellors** — the credentialed adults whom
ADHD/ASD families already trust for tool recommendations.

## 1. Case studies — how indie family-tech actually broke in

### OurFamilyWizard — the gold standard (and closest match to our situation)
- Founder **Jai Kissoon** + mother **Kathleen Kissoon** (Minneapolis family-law
  attorney). Launched 2001. **Bootstrapped ~19 years** before the first equity
  (Spectrum Equity growth investment, Apr 2020). ~1M cumulative users by then.
  ([OFW history](https://www.ourfamilywizard.com/about-us/history),
  [Spectrum Equity](https://www.spectrumequity.com/news/spectrum-equity-invests-in-ourfamilywizard/))
- **Wedge:** court-admissible, tamper-evident messaging for *high-conflict
  divorced co-parents* — not couples, not intact families. Immutable,
  timestamped, exportable as evidence. ToneMeter flags hostile phrasing.
- **First ~1,000 — the killer channel:** In **Nov 2002, Hennepin County
  family-court Judge James Swenson** ran a pilot — took the 40 most
  high-conflict custody families, **court-ordered them onto OFW**, and they
  "disappeared from the court system." That single judge-led pilot became the
  national model. OFW now publishes
  [Model Order Language](https://www.ourfamilywizard.com/practitioners/model-order-language)
  for judges to paste into custody orders; hundreds of judges in all 50 states
  order it from the bench. ([JAMS bio of Swenson](https://www.jamsadr.com/neutrals/swenson))
- **GTM:** sells to *judges, mediators, GALs, family-law attorneys* via a
  [Practitioners portal](https://www.ourfamilywizard.com/practitioners), CLE
  credits, and NCJFCJ corporate sponsorship — practitioners then mandate the
  product to parents who *must* pay. Near-zero consumer paid acquisition in the
  bootstrapped era.
- **Sacrificed:** the entire happy-family TAM to own the divorce courtroom.
- **Pricing:** $99–$144/yr — same band as us.
- **Takeaway:** a niche-within-a-niche, sold via a credentialed gatekeeper who
  effectively mandates payment. This is the template.

### Gabb / Troomi / Pinwheel — the kid-phone cohort (all VC-funded)
- **Gabb** (Stephen Dalby, 2018): "dumb by design" hardware bundle — no
  internet/social/app-store. Indiegogo 244% ($122k, 2019) → word-of-mouth →
  **LDS/Utah faith + homeschool network** (circumstantial but strong: Lehi HQ,
  BYU founder, Series A co-led by ex-BYU QB Taysom Hill). 471% growth 2020,
  $14M Series A Apr 2021. ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210421005383/en/))
- **Troomi** (Bill Brady, 2021): "grows-with-the-kid" — starts dumb, parents
  unlock vetted apps. **The faith-community channel is so valuable Gabb SUED
  Troomi (Jan 2023, Utah federal court)** alleging misuse of customer lists and
  "faith-community distribution relationships." Brady (ex-Gabb, BYU student-body
  president, 20-yr marketer) ran a **Christian-parenting podcast saturation
  play** (Scrolling 2 Death, Shaped by Faith, Raising Christian Kids, etc.).
  375% CAGR first 36 months. $13.5M+ raised.
  ([Gabb v. Troomi complaint](https://business.cch.com/ipld/GabbWirelessTroomiWireless20230118.pdf))
- **Pinwheel** (Dane Witbeck, 2019): "first *smartphone* for kids" — full
  Android + caregiver portal. ~30k phones / $11M revenue / ~$2M net loss by
  mid-2023. $3.5M seed Jan 2022 + Wefunder community round at $26M valuation.
  Heavy **founder podcast tour** (Code Story, SXSW Pitch finalist, Practical
  Ecommerce). ([Kingscrowd](https://kingscrowd.com/pinwheel-on-wefunder-2023/))

**Cross-cutting pattern:** all four sacrifice the broad TAM to dominate a
credentialed niche (OFW = courts; Gabb = LDS families; Troomi = Christian
podcast-parents; Pinwheel = engaged-parent communities). The single
highest-leverage channel across all four is **founder-as-evangelist on niche
podcasts** — cheap, repeatable, durable.

### Privacy/productivity bootstrappers (analogous economics)
- **Cryptee** (John Ozbay, 2018): *"After a year of coding… I made a post about
  Cryptee on Reddit, and got 20,000 users overnight."* Bootstrapped, profitable,
  deliberately collects no analytics. Biggest regret: under-invested in
  marketing early. ([IdeaMensch](https://ideamensch.com/john-ozbay/))
- **one sec** (Frederik Riedel, bootstrapped): single launch screen-recording
  tweet (~700 followers) → Instagram creators → earned PR (Stanford, Cambridge,
  PNAS paper, TIME) → App Store ranking flywheel. Hit ~$10k MRR before first
  hire; now 18 people. ([Launched Ep 89](https://launchedfm.com/episode/89-one-sec-frederik-riedel))
- **Obsidian** (Steph Ango): ~$25M ARR, ~1.5M MAU, **7-person team, no VC, no
  analytics**, valuation ~$350M. Proof local-first/privacy converts — on a
  smaller funnel (~$16/user/yr). ([Decoder, Aug 2025](https://finance.biggo.com/news/iVboYp0Bga3fZL9MJEv_))

### Failure cases (what the misread looks like)
- **Cydoc** (7-yr health-AI shutdown, 2026): inbound (SEO/blog/podcast
  tour/conferences) produced **ZERO** customers over 7 years; only outbound
  landed the 4 it ever had. Founder's #1 stated mistake: *"Not enough
  customers."* ([post-mortem](https://glassboxmedicine.com/2026/02/21/why-i-shut-down-my-bootstrapped-health-ai-startup-after-7-years-a-founders-postmortem/))
- **Skiff** (E2E-privacy productivity, acquihired into Notion 2024 after $14M
  raised): privacy wedge converted enthusiasts but never crossed to paid
  mainstream. ([Notesnook autopsy](https://blog.notesnook.com/the-skiff-privacy-fiasco))
- **The notes-app VC trap** (Alex MacCaw / Reflect): VC forces collaboration
  features → product becomes a team tool → loses the personal wedge.

> **No verifiable founder-written post-mortem of a bootstrapped solo
> parental-control SaaS exists in the public record.** That absence is itself a
> signal — these die quietly (App Store delisting, blog goes dark), which means
> the category is unforgiving and the survivors are the embedded-niche players.

## 2. Community-led growth — the empirical reality

### Show HN / Hacker News
- Front-page #1–#5 delivers **5k–25k visits in 24h**; **1–3% signup**;
  **0–15 paying customers week 1**.
- **Consumer/lifestyle launches consistently flop.** Aidlab (wearable):
  front page, **0 conversions**. No documented parenting/fitness Show HN
  success exists. Dev tools dominate every win.
  ([Aidlab post-mortem](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/front-page-of-hn-the-full-postmortem-traffic-lessons-surprises-cbe9e0a7f6))
- **Implication: Show HN is not for us** (parents aren't on HN).

### Product Hunt (2026)
- Median small-SaaS outcome: **56 signups, $0 paid** (Raport, Jan 2025);
  another: **5 upvotes, 1 signup, $0** (May 2026). Credible upper bound for an
  indie: **107 paying customers** (Reverb). Audience now mostly other founders
  reciprocal-upvoting. ([Edward Sturm data](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/product-hunt-results/))
- **Implication: one-shot vanity spike, not a business.**

### Reddit
- High variance, high ban risk: **89% of startup marketing attempts banned
  within 30 days**. r/Parenting (8.2M) heavily moderated, no promo exception.
  Best case found: 48 conversions in 2 weeks from one non-viral post.
- **r/ADHD_Parenting (45k) is the realistic target** — but verify wiki rules
  in-browser (Reddit blocks fetching them) and participate for months, no spam.

### Discord — the one community channel with a real benchmark
- Hobby Discord (3D-printing minis): **3.3% paid conversion** at $7/mo →
  $700 MRR. DevTools Discord: 2,500 members → £18k MRR.
  ([case study](https://kisoid.medium.com/case-study-from-a-simple-discord-server-to-a-profitable-community-business-a357b5d55512))
- **Implication: build/own an ADHD-parent Discord; ~3% paid is the planning
  number.**

### Build-in-public (X/Twitter)
- Works, but the audience is *other founders*. Damon Chen: **80-90% of early
  customers from Twitter** — because his customers ARE founders. Marc Lou's
  ShipFast sells to developers. **Our customers (ADHD parents) are not on
  founder-Twitter**, so build-in-public is weak for us as a *sales* channel
  (fine as a personal-brand/recruiting channel).

### Facebook parenting groups
- **No documented founder win exists.** Heavily moderated; expect removal on
  first post. Treat as a dead direct-promo channel.

## 3. Privacy positioning — strong but bounded

- **Proton:** 100M+ accounts (Apr 2023), profitable, **no VC since 2021**, now
  a nonprofit Foundation. Paid count undisclosed (~4–5M / ~4-5% conversion
  estimated). ([proton.me](https://proton.me/blog/proton-100-million-accounts))
- **DuckDuckGo:** **plateaued** — ~100M queries/day flat for 4 years; US share
  down ~31% from its 2.42% peak to 1.67%; revenue stuck at "$100M+" since 2020.
  ([Backlinko/StatCounter](https://backlinko.com/duckduckgo-stats))
- **Standard Notes:** pure E2EE/OSS capped at **300K users** then exited to
  Proton (2024).
- **Mullvad / IVPN:** deliberately never disclose subscribers (consistent with
  privacy stance) — stay niche. **NordVPN** ($3B valuation) wins the VPN market
  via **40% affiliate commissions**, not privacy purity.
- **Signal:** runs **$38M/yr costs net-negative** on Brian Acton's $105M
  forgiveness loan; ~2-3% of WhatsApp scale.

**Verdict:** privacy/local-first builds a *real but bounded* business
($5–25M ARR, 0.3–3M users, sub-100-person team). It does **not** break into the
mainstream against networked incumbents. **Pair it with the PC-first wedge;
don't lead with it.** Supporting fact that helps the pitch: **70% of
parental-control apps share kid data without consent** (EPFL/UCL), and **no
incumbent leads on privacy** — open lane, just not a standalone growth engine.

## 4. The PC-first wedge — the real moat

This is the strongest part of the entire research.

- **Microsoft Family Safety is actively degrading:** removed location/driving
  features **Nov 29, 2024**; a **June 2024 bug crashed Chrome on kid accounts
  for weeks** with no timely patch; reliability complaints widespread. It's free
  but feature-thin and broken.
  ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/windows-operating-systems/windows-parental-controls-are-crashing-chrome-heres-the-workaround))
- **Apple Screen Time = zero Windows coverage.** Apple-only by definition.
- **No paid competitor leads on PC/gaming.** Bark/Qustodio treat Windows as an
  afterthought; the family conflict for 9–14yo kids lives on the gaming PC
  (Steam, Discord, Minecraft, Roblox).

**The gaming PC is the seam: Microsoft is shedding features there, Apple has
nothing, and every paid competitor de-prioritises it. That is our defensible
position.**

## 5. Validation thresholds — the go/no-go numbers

- **$79/yr placement:** mid-market — exactly Net Nanny's family tier, ~20% under
  Bark/Qustodio flagship ($99), ~30% over Mobicip Standard. Correct price.
- **Trial→paid (RevenueCat 2026):** median **10.7%** Day-35 (hard paywall);
  2.1% (freemium-with-trial). Higher-priced apps convert better.
- **Annual Year-1 cancellation (RevenueCat 2026):** **~72% median** (up from 56%
  in 2025); upper quartile retains 60–75%. ~30% cancel in month 1.
- **Best leading indicator (Amplitude):** **Day-7 retention ≥ 7% predicts
  top-quartile 3-month retention with ~69% concordance.** This is the cleanest
  30-day go/no-go test we have. ([Amplitude 7% rule](https://amplitude.com/blog/7-percent-retention-rule))
- **PMF shape (Balfour/Winters/a16z):** the cohort retention curve must visibly
  *flatten*, not hit an absolute height. Watch the W4→W8 slope.

**Our 30-day gate: Day-7 retention ≥ 7%.** If yes → keep going. If no → pivot
or kill before sinking months in.

## 6. Regulation — NOT a tailwind (honest reversal)

The earlier optimistic read in `MARKETING.md` was wrong. The detailed
regulatory research says:

- **EU DSA Art. 28 + Commission Guidelines (Jul 2025):** the duty falls on
  **platforms**, not third-party tools. Parental controls treated as a built-in
  platform feature.
- **EU Digital Identity (EUDI) mini-wallet (launching summer 2026):** Baker
  McKenzie states it gives platforms a harmonised age-gating tool, "eliminating
  extensive costs for third-party identification services" — i.e. it
  **disintermediates** third-party age-verification vendors.
- **UK Ofcom Children's Codes (in force Jul 25, 2025):** duties on platforms;
  parental controls framed as a complementary built-in feature.

**These regimes commoditise the function (age-gating into EUDI rails, parental
controls into mandatory platform built-ins). They do NOT create a regulated
third-party market the way PSD2 did for open banking.** Narrow surviving
niches: independent auditing, cross-platform parent dashboards, on-device
overlays — none mandated. **Do not bet the GTM on a regulatory tailwind.**

## 7. Market context (sizing + incumbents)

- **TAM:** ~$1.55–2.24B (2024–25) → 9.6–12.3% CAGR → ~$4B by 2034 (multiple
  firms; Fortune Business Insights, Strategic Market Research, Zion, GGI).
- **Incumbent pricing:** Bark $99/yr · Qustodio $54.95–$109.95 · Net Nanny
  $79.99 (5 devices) · Mobicip $35.88–$95.88 · Aura Family ~$384/yr · Apple
  Screen Time / Google Family Link free.
- **Aura:** ~$385M ARR, $1.6B valuation, ~50% YoY growth (2024).
- **Qustodio:** acquired by Qoria (ASX) for $52M (2022); 4M users / 180
  countries.
- **Bark:** ~$2.37M US mobile-app revenue 2024 (Statista; software-only slice).
- **Demand base:** 66% of US parents use parental controls (Pew); 81% of
  under-13s have their own device; Apple Screen Time cited at 50M+ families.

### ADHD-family economics (our beachhead)
- Comparable ADHD tools: Inflow ~$16.50/mo · Numo $59.99/yr · Tiimo $7–12/mo ·
  Shimmer coaching $140–345/mo.
- **ADDitude survey (11,013 responses): ADHD families prioritise efficacy and
  fit over price.** Affordability + low-friction onboarding drive adoption;
  human accountability commands premium prices. Supports $79/yr + a real "fits
  our chaotic life" story over a cheap-and-cheerful pitch.

## 8. The decision

1. **Lead with the PC wedge:** "the only good parental control for the gaming
   PC." Microsoft is degrading; Apple has nothing; competitors ignore it.
2. **Pair with privacy as secondary:** "and we don't sell your kid's data."
   Differentiator, not a standalone engine.
3. **Beachhead = ADHD/ASD households with a Windows gaming PC.** They churn
   least, value fit over price, and have credentialed gatekeepers who recommend
   tools.
4. **Find your Judge Swenson:** ONE ADHD coach / pediatrician / child
   psychologist / school counsellor with reach, embedded as a champion. This is
   the highest-leverage move in the entire plan.
5. **Channels for the first 90 days:** founder-on-niche-podcasts (ADHD/parenting
   circuit) + one owned ADHD-parent Discord (~3% paid benchmark) + the one
   gatekeeper. **Skip SEO, paid ads, Show HN, Product Hunt.**
6. **30-day validation gate:** Day-7 retention ≥ 7%. Continue if hit; pivot/kill
   if not.
7. **Do NOT bank on regulation.** It commoditises, not opens.
