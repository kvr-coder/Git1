What your parent can see

No secrets here — this is everything timeoff shows your parent. We told them you'd see this list too.

  • How many minutes you used today (not which websites)
  • Which apps you launched (just names — Discord, Minecraft — not chats)
  • When the PC was locked or unlocked
  • Time requests and chores you sent
  • Your bank balance and what you spent it on

What your parent does NOT see

  • Your messages, chats, or DMs
  • Your screen contents or screenshots
  • Which websites you visited
  • Your search history

If you think a rule is unfair, send a request with a reason — the chat is the point. Research shows kids who talk about limits with their parents end up with more freedom, not less.
